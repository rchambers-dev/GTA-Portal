# Management read-out example — Autocare six-KSB shadow pilot

**Audience:** curriculum leadership / directors  
**Status:** illustrative narrative from the human-reviewed six-KSB shadow pilot only  
**Not:** an overall programme score  
**Not:** production programme truth  

---

## How the Portal can explain programme health (worked example)

### GOOD

**K12 — Braking systems (Primary B2 kept)**  
Learners progress from basic brake understanding through replacement practice to advanced ABS. Ownership is clear. Later advanced work reinforces; it does not redefine the home.

**K21 — Electric/hybrid service, charging and safety (Primary B6 kept)**  
The first substantive service/safety home remains the right structural home. Earlier awareness and later specialist reinforcement sit around it, rather than replacing it.

---

### GOOD BUT EVIDENCE THINNER

**K23 — High-voltage safe working (Primary B6 kept)**  
Safe-working and isolation **rules** are taught. Structural ownership remains sound. Practice and assessment evidence currently look thinner than the teaching and should be strengthened or better evidenced — without treating the KSB as “unmapped”.

---

### TEACHING EXISTS BUT STRUCTURE IS WRONG / INCOMPLETE

**S9 — Tyre remove, repair, replace and balance**  
The live curriculum clearly teaches removal, repair, refitting and balancing. This is no longer defensibly a simple “not taught” gap. What remains open is Primary ownership and fuller visibility of assessed practical competence.

**K10 — Steering systems**  
Mechanical, hydraulic and EPS/EPAS content is substantively taught, with confirmed practical application. Ownership across earlier teaching Blocks versus later practical application is not yet settled. Strong later practical must not automatically become Primary.

---

### STRUCTURE IS RIGHT BUT COMPETENCE EVIDENCE IS WEAK

**S13 — Isolate high-voltage systems for maintenance (Primary B6 retained under review)**  
Structural Primary at B6 remains appropriate for now. Teaching of isolation **rules** is visible. Assessed learner demonstration of isolation **competence** is not yet proven in the supplied corpus. Rules taught ≠ competence assessed.

---

## Why this narrative matters

A single mapped/gap flag would wrongly either:

- mark S9/K10 as empty curriculum when teaching is present, or  
- treat S13 as fully healthy because a Primary exists, or  
- tempt remapping K12/K21 to later/stronger appearance of evidence.

Multi-axis assurance lets leadership see:

| Category | KSBs in this pilot |
|---|---|
| Good | K12, K21 |
| Good but evidence thinner | K23 |
| Teaching exists; structure incomplete | S9, K10 |
| Structure right; competence evidence weak | S13 |

No aggregate score is offered. The point of this example is that the story is understandable without reducing curriculum quality to one traffic light.
