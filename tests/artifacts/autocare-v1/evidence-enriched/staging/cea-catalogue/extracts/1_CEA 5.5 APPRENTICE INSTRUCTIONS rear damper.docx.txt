 Materials you need at the workstation:
 Light vehicle .

 Scenario:
 You are required to remove and re fit the rear suspension damper your assessor instructs you to change, as reported during a vehicle safety inspection.

 Complete the following tasks and note your results where indicated:
 Ensure your working environment is safe and the vehicle is correctly prepared before starting work . Jack/list safely if required .
 Remove and re fit the rear suspension damper .
 Demonstrate basic checks on the damper to your assessor.
 Tighten all components to 50% (half) of the torque setting specified ensuring you record the CORRECT settings in the Apprentice Assessment Record sheet
 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation to a pre -assessment condition .
