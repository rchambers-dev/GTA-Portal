 Objectives:
 To ensure safe working practices are followed throughout your assessment .
 To demonstrate a methodical procedure when carrying out removal and replacement of steering system components

 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to remove and re place steering system components .
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to reinstate the vehicle to the pre-assessment condition .
 To correctly complete the Apprentice Assessment Record including details specific to the system worked on during your assessment .

 Materials you need at the workstation:
 Light vehicle
 Replacement track rod end.
 Manufacturer’s instructions and technical information relevant for the assessment
 General and specialist tools and equipment made available by your assessor relevant for the task .
 Apprentice Assessment Record sheet

 Scenario:
 You are required to remove and replace the track rod end your assessor instructs you to change, as reported during a vehicle safety inspection.

 Complete the following tasks and note your results where indicated:
 Ensure your working environment is safe and the vehicle is correctly prepared before starting work . Jack vehicle up safely if required.
 Remove and re place track rod end .
 Tighten all components to 50% (half) of the torque setting specified ensuring you record the CORRECT settings in the Apprentice Assessment Record sheet
 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation to a pre -assessment condition .
