APPRENTICE INSTRUCTIONS
 Ref: 14
 Vehicle Inspection ( 45 mins)

 Objectives:

 To ensure safe working practices are followed throughout your vehicle safety inspection
 To demonstrate a methodical workshop procedure when carrying out a vehicle safety inspection
 To demonstrate your vehicle knowledge and vehicle inspection techniques
 To correctly complete a vehicle inspection sheet recording any vehicle and system defects identified

 Materials you need at the workstation:

 Vehicle with 5 inserted defects (ideally no other defects)
 Vehicle Safety Inspection Checklist
 Inspection tools made available by your assessor relevant for the task

 Complete the following tasks and note your results where indicated: The vehicle that you are about to inspect has just arrived at your workplace.

 Carry out all necessary procedures required to care for the vehicle before, during and after the inspection
 Carry out a vehicle inspection as detailed in the vehicle safety inspection checklist
 Complete the vehicle safety inspection checklist, including all vehicle details and reporting any vehicle defects found during the inspection

 Note. Do not repair any defects identified
