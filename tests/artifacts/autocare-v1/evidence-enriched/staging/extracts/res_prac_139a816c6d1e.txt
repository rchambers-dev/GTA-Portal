Your task
A customer reports slow cranking and intermittent starting concerns. You must carry out basic battery and
charging system checks, record results and explain whether further work is required.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and confirm the complaint with the assessor/workplace mentor.
2 Inspect battery security, terminals and visible condition.
3 Measure battery voltage and charging voltage as instructed.
4 Record results and compare with manufacturer or accepted values.
5 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation to a safe, clean condition.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle with 12V battery
• Multimeter or battery tester
• Manufacturer technical information
• PPE and battery handling equipment
• Apprentice Assessment Record
