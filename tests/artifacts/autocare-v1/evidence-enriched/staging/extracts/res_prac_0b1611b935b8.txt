Your task
A vehicle has been reported with a worn suspension component. You must inspect the component, remove/refit
the part as instructed, record torque data and identify whether alignment or further checks are required.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and raise/support it safely where required.
2 Inspect the relevant suspension component and confirm the fault.
3 Remove and refit the component instructed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
4 Record correct torque settings and any alignment implications.
5 Reinstate the vehicle/workstation and explain recommendations.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or suspension rig
• Replacement component as required
• Manufacturer technical information
• Torque wrench and general tools
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
