Your task
A vehicle has an electrical concern requiring structured testing. You must carry out safe electrical checks, record
values and explain whether the evidence confirms the fault or requires further diagnosis.
What you must do
1 Confirm the reported electrical concern with the assessor/workplace mentor.
2 Prepare the vehicle/rig and select safe test points.
3 Carry out the electrical tests instructed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
4 Record results and compare with expected values.
5 Explain whether the evidence confirms the fault and reinstate the workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or electrical training rig
• Multimeter and test leads
• Manufacturer wiring or technical information
• Battery support/PPE as required
• Apprentice Assessment Record
