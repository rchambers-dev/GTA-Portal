 Topic
 Fire Safety, Battery Awareness and Basic Electrical Safety
 Occupational relevance: This session develops fire prevention, battery safety and basic electrical risk awareness. Applications include fuel and solvent risks, 12/24-volt systems, charging, welding equipment, spray-booth controls, workshop electrical tools and safe use of a multimeter within defined limits.
 Lesson aims
 • Recognise fire classes, common causes and suitable controls.
 • Understand lead-acid, lithium and low-voltage battery hazards.
 • Develop safe basic multimeter use and electrical-risk reporting.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Explain the fire triangle and identify suitable extinguisher types.
 • Identify battery and charging hazards and required PPE/ventilation.
 • Use a multimeter safely for a simple voltage or resistance measurement.
 • State when electrical work must stop and be escalated.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Fire-sign/extinguisher matching and battery-hazard questions.
 • During: Observation of safe set-up, measurement and response decisions.
 • End: Fire/battery checklist, recorded reading and one escalation scenario.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
