Your task
A customer asks for advice after an EV/hybrid service or warning message. You must complete non-invasive
checks, prepare a customer handover explanation and identify safe referral points.
What you must do
1 Confirm the customer concern and check available vehicle information.
2 Identify visible EV/hybrid components, warning labels and charging information.
3 Record any non-invasive observations or advisory notes.
4 Prepare a customer handover explanation including limitations.
5 Explain referral requirements and complete documentation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• EV/hybrid vehicle, rig or case-study pack
• Charging information or customer FAQ sheet
• Manufacturer safety information
• HV awareness PPE where appropriate
• Apprentice Assessment Record
