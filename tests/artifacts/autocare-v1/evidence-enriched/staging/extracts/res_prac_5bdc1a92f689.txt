Your task
You must complete a capstone practical task that brings together technical work, safe working, documentation,
quality checks and professional explanation. The task should be agreed with the assessor/workplace mentor
before starting.
What you must do
1 Agree the capstone task scope with the assessor/workplace mentor.
2 Prepare the vehicle/rig, tools, technical information and safety controls.
3 Complete the task following the agreed method statement.
4 Carry out quality checks and record evidence.
5 Explain the outcome and complete final documentation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or approved training rig
• Task brief agreed with assessor/workplace mentor
• Relevant tools and technical information
• QA checklist and job card
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
