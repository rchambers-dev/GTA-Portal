 Basic Air Conditioning System 
 A compressor forces a liquid, called refrigerant, around the A/C system in pipes under pressure. 
 
 The evaporator is located inside the passenger compartment to remove heat. 
 
 The condenser is located in an air flow, which is normally at the front of the vehicle. 
 
 The condenser dissipates heat away from the system and into the surrounding air.

Compressor 
 
 The Compressor 
 
 The compressor pulls in low pressure refrigerant vapour from the evaporator and pumps it out at high pressure. 
 
 This pressurization raises the temperature and the boiling point of refrigerant vapour going into the condenser.

 The Condenser 
 
 The condenser is usually located at the front of the vehicle and receives high pressure refrigerant vapour from the compressor. 
 
 The vapour is cooled by ram (forced-in) air, which changes it into a high-pressure liquid. 
 
 A restriction between the condenser and the evaporator changes the liquid from high to low pressure, lowering its boiling point.

Air Conditioning and Climate Control Systems 
 
 Air conditioning and climate control systems keep a constant, ideal temperature inside your car, ensuring you always stay cool and comfortable. 
 
 The air conditioning system complements the heating and ventilation system in your car. 
 
 It includes a cooling system, heating, humidity controller and blower unit, this all works just like the heat pump in a refrigerator. 
 
 In simple terms, the air is directed through an evaporator, where it loses its heat to the refrigerant. The cooled and dried air is then blown into the cabin of your car. A compressor cools the refrigerant down to the required temperature.

Climatronic Automatic Air Conditioning 
 
 Climatronic regulates the temperature of the cabin based on the chosen temperature setting. It adjusts the blower speed and operates the air distribution flaps automatically. This ensures a pleasant interior climate in all conditions, with minimal draught as the air flows through the passenger compartment. 
 The Climatronic systems electronic control monitors all the key factors, such as the position of the sun, the intensity of its rays and the outside temperature. It will then add more, or less cool air accordingly. 
 Climatronic also includes a pushbutton defrost function to clear fogged or iced-up windscreens. This directs the full airflow onto the inside of the glass at the highest blower and heat output settings. 
 If the outside temperature is above 0°C, the cooling system cuts in as well to dry the air before it is heated.

Heater Faults 
 
 Many aspects of the heater system can malfunction which require repair. 
 
 A cabin filter is used to clean impurities from the air before it enters the ventilation system, when this filter becomes blocked it can slow the airflow in which case the filter needs replacement. 
 
 An engine coolant leak can develop which hinders the performance of the heater system, these leaks must be repaired before proper heater operation will return. 
 
 When a heater core fails, a coolant residue may be observed upon the passenger side floorboard, this will typically be accompanied by a sweet, pungent odour .

Testing For Leaks 
 
 Checking for leaks in a hot cooling system can be hazardous as there is a risk of burning yourself. 
 
 To simulate the pressure of a hot system, but at a cold temperature, you should use a pressure tester. 
 
 This allows you to apply a pressure to the system by removing the pressure cap and fitting the gauge.

Heater Faults 
 
 Some vehicles use a heater control valve which is used to stop the coolant from flowing into the heater core when the heater system is not being used. 
 
 If an audible bubbling noise is heard from the heater core area, it is due to air being trapped inside the heater core, in time, the air should dissipate to silence the noise.
