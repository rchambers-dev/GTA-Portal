Your task
As part of final readiness, you must complete an integrated practical inspection under mock assessment
conditions. You will inspect, record evidence, explain findings and identify any further work or authorisation
needed.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and work area safely.
2 Complete the integrated inspection sequence set by the assessor/workplace mentor.
3 Record all technical measurements and defect findings.
4 Explain priorities, customer recommendations and authorisation requirements.
5 Complete final documentation and reinstate the workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle with selected defects
• Inspection checklist and job card
• Measuring tools and torque wrench
• Manufacturer technical information
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
