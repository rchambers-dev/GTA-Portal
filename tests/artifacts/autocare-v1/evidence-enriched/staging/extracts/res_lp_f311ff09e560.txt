 Topic
 Measurement, Technical Information and Tolerances
 Occupational relevance: This session develops accurate measurement, units, calibration awareness, technical-data use and tolerance. Applications include brake discs and tread depth, heavy-vehicle hubs and pressures, paint ratios/viscosity/temperature, panel alignment/gaps and autocare inspection data.
 Lesson aims
 • Develop accurate use of common measuring tools and units.
 • Understand datum points, tolerances, calibration and quality requirements.
 • Apply technical information and record results clearly.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Select and use at least three measuring tools correctly.
 • Convert or interpret relevant metric/imperial values.
 • Compare measurements with a specification or tolerance.
 • Complete a clear measured-results record and identify corrective action.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Estimate-and-measure starter and questions on units/tolerance.
 • During: Observed measurement technique, reading checks and feedback.
 • End: Completed measurement sheet with comparison to specification.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
