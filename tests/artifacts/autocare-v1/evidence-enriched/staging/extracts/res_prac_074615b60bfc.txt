Your task
During routine workshop activity, a fluid spill and several waste items are identified. You must make the area
safe, identify waste streams, record the incident and explain how environmental controls prevent harm.
What you must do
1 Assess the work area and identify immediate risks.
2 Select PPE and control the spill or simulated spill as instructed.
3 Identify correct waste stream controls for fluids and contaminated materials.
4 Complete an incident or environmental record.
5 Explain corrective actions and reinstate the work area.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Workshop area or simulated spill scenario
• Spill kit and signage
• COSHH information or safety data sheets
• Waste stream labels/containers
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
