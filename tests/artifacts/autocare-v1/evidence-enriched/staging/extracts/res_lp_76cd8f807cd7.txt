 Topic
 Technical Documentation, Job Cards, Handover and Specialist Transition
 Occupational relevance: This session develops accurate technical records, job-card/report writing, handover communication and readiness to progress into specialist curriculum. Examples reflect service, inspection, heavy-vehicle, refinishing, panel-repair and autocare workplace documentation.
 Lesson aims
 • Consolidate accurate technical and workplace documentation.
 • Develop clear job-card, report and handover communication.
 • Review occupational learning and prepare for specialist programme delivery.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Identify the essential information in a strong job card or technical report.
 • Complete a clear pathway-relevant job card/report using suitable terminology.
 • Give a concise verbal or written handover and respond to feedback.
 • Agree one specialist learning action and understand the next programme stage.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Compare strong/weak documentation examples and identify omissions.
 • During: Review writing, questioning and feedback followed by correction.
 • End: Completed improved job card/report and transition action.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
