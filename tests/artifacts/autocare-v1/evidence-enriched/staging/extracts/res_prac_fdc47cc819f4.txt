Your task
A vehicle is booked for an interim service. You must follow the service schedule, identify required service items,
complete the inspection documentation and record any additional work requiring customer authorisation.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and workstation safely.
2 Identify required service items and technical information.
3 Carry out service checks as instructed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
4 Record findings, parts required and any additional work.
5 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation and complete documentation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle
• Service schedule
• Oil filter/air filter examples where required
• Fluid measuring/checking equipment
• Proforma/job card documentation
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
