 Topic
 Workshop Safety, Hazards, Housekeeping and Occupational Expectations
 Occupational relevance: This shared occupational session develops safe work-area preparation, hazard controls, equipment responsibility, communication and professional workshop behaviours. Pathway examples are used for Light Vehicle, Heavy Vehicle, Paint, Panel and Autocare apprentices.
 Lesson aims
 • Develop safe and professional working expectations for the training centre and workplace.
 • Recognise common workshop hazards and select suitable controls.
 • Understand responsibilities for housekeeping, tools, communication and escalation.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Identify at least five hazards and state an appropriate control for each.
 • Explain common safety signs, emergency points and reporting routes.
 • Complete a concise safe-work-area or hazard record using clear terminology.
 • Contribute effectively to a team safety task and identify one workplace action.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Baseline questions and a short safety-sign or hazard-identification task.
 • During: Observation, targeted questioning and corrective feedback.
 • End: Completed hazard record plus one exit question on workplace application.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.

 Workplace follow-up
