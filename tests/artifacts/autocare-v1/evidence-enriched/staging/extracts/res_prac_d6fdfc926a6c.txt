PROGRAMME POINT

Your task
A vehicle has been reported with worn brake components during inspection. You must inspect, measure and
remove/refit brake components, record technical data and explain any customer recommendations.
What you must do
1 Ensure your working environment is safe and the vehicle is correctly prepared before starting work.
2 Carry out a brake inspection, including relevant measurements of brake discs, pads and visible components.
3 Remove and replace the brake components instructed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
4 Tighten all safety-critical components to the full manufacturer-specified torque and record the settings used.
5 Record the correct manufacturer torque settings and any customer recommendations.
6 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation to a safe, clean condition.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle
• Replacement braking components as required
• Manufacturer technical information
• Brake measuring tools, torque wrench and general tools
• Dial gauge if disc run-out is included
• Apprentice Assessment Record
