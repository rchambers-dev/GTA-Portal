Your task
A vehicle has had alignment, tyre or body-related work and may require ADAS checks. You must complete
non-invasive pre-calibration checks, record findings and identify whether specialist calibration referral is required.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and identify visible ADAS features.
2 Carry out pre-checks for tyres, ride height, alignment and visible sensor damage.
3 Record findings and compare with available guidance.
4 Decide whether the vehicle is suitable for calibration or requires correction/referral.
5 Explain the outcome to the assessor/workplace mentor and complete documentation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or ADAS case-study pack
• Manufacturer guidance or sample calibration report
• Tyre pressure gauge/alignment data where available
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
