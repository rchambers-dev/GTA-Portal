 Topic
 Health and Safety, Tools, Equipment and Safe Vehicle Support
 Occupational relevance: This session develops safe selection, inspection and use of tools and equipment, including safe lifting and supporting. Examples include light-vehicle jacks and torque tools, heavy-vehicle lifts, raised inspection systems and high-capacity equipment, paint preparation equipment, panel repair systems and autocare fast-fit tools.
 Lesson aims
 • Reinforce health and safety responsibilities and safe workshop conduct.
 • Develop safe pre-use inspection and selection of tools and equipment.
 • Introduce safe lifting, supporting, SWL and torque-control principles.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Explain SWL and why equipment condition and calibration matter.
 • Complete a dynamic risk assessment for a lifting or support task.
 • Select and use appropriate tools safely under supervision.
 • Record equipment checks and identify when equipment must be removed from use.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Retrieval questions from FS01 and a tool-condition sorting task.
 • During: Tutor observation, questioning and correction during demonstrations and practical work.
 • End: Completed risk assessment/tool check and verbal explanation of one safety-critical control.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
