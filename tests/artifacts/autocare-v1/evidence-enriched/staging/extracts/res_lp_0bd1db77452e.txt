 Topic and intended learning
 Lesson aims - Develop secure occupational knowledge and safe working practice for multi-line estimates and customer choice conversations. - Connect the session to workplace quality, communication and customer/business impact. - Produce clear evidence that can be reviewed and improved.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to - Explain the key principles, sequence, risks and controls for multi-line estimates and customer choice conversations. - Complete the planned practical, scenario or documentation activity safely and accurately. - Record findings and justify decisions using technical information or workplace procedures.
 How learning will be checked - Baseline and retrieval questions at the start. - Observation, questioning and review during activity. - Feedback, correction and an end-of-session impact check. - Formal assessment, gateway and EPA activity remain separate.
 Inclusion, support and stretch - Use visual prompts, glossaries, staged checklists and model examples where needed. - Adjust access arrangements without lowering occupational expectations. - Stretch: justify alternatives, evaluate risk/quality and explain workplace impact.
 RPL and individual training plan control - Deliver the full master plan unless an approved RPL adjustment changes the learner’s block plan. - Record only the eligible OTJ actually delivered. Omitted or shortened learning must agree with Excel and the portal.
 Impact and follow-up - Identify what the apprentice can now explain or complete with greater confidence and independence. - Record one workplace application or evidence action. - Review progress against the block objectives and agree the next step.

 Detailed theory session covering multi-line estimates and how to present several repair options clearly and ethically. The tutor explains how a quotation may include safety-critical work, advisory work, preventative maintenance and customer-choice items.
 Baseline questions; correct misconceptions. Support with visuals/glossary. Stretch: connect principles to workplace risk and quality.

 Structured estimate-building activity using inspection findings from realistic scenarios. Learners create multi-line estimates that separate urgent repairs, advisory recommendations and optional work.
 Observe process and safe working. Support with staged checklist. Stretch: justify method and compare alternatives.

 12:45-14:15 1.50 OTJ
 Application activity where learners practise customer choice conversations through role-play. Learners present the multi-line estimate, explain the difference between required and recommended work, answer customer questions and respond to cost concerns.
 Review output and reasoning. Support with model examples. Stretch: evaluate quality, efficiency and customer/business impact.
 Estimate templates; labour guides; parts pricing; approval forms

 Excluded from OTJ.

 14:30-16:00 1.50 OTJ
 Consolidation through review of estimate quality, customer explanation and authorisation records. The tutor questions learners on how they prioritised work, how they explained options and how they avoided misleading the customer. Learners refine weak wording and improve any unclear estimate lines.
 Exit check and feedback. Confirm evidence action. Stretch: explain improvement and transfer to the workplace.
 Evidence: Quotation documents; customer approval forms; labour/parts breakdowns
 Workplace follow-up Apply one aspect of the session in the workplace and retain a job card, observation, photograph, mentor comment or other suitable evidence where available.
