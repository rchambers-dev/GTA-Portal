Your task
A vehicle has been reported with possible tyre and brake wear during a basic workshop check. You must inspect
visible brake and tyre components, record measurements and explain any customer recommendations.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and work area safely.
2 Inspect tyre condition, tread depth, pressure and visible damage.
3 Inspect visible brake components and record condition.
4 Record wheel torque setting and any relevant technical data.
5 Explain findings to the assessor/workplace mentor and reinstate the workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle
• Tread depth gauge and tyre pressure gauge
• Wheel brace/torque wrench
• Brake inspection tools
• Manufacturer technical information
• Apprentice Assessment Record
