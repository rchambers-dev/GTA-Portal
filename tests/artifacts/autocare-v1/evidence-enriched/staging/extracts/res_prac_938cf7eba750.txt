Your task
A customer vehicle has arrived for an initial safety inspection. You must prepare the work area, use PPE correctly,
complete a structured inspection and record any safety-related findings without carrying out repairs.
What you must do
1 Ensure the working area is safe and the vehicle is correctly prepared before starting work.
2 Carry out external, under-bonnet, in-vehicle and raised-vehicle checks as instructed.
3 Record all inspection findings using clear workshop terminology.
4 Identify any safety-critical defects and explain them to the assessor/workplace mentor.
5 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation to a safe, clean condition.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle with inserted inspection defects
• Vehicle inspection checklist
• Torch, tread depth gauge and tyre pressure gauge
• PPE and workshop safety equipment
• Apprentice Assessment Record
