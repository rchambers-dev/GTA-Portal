5. Assertive 
 
 
 Explain what you think Assertive means!.................

Assertive, is being confident in yourself, being confident about your facts, presenting your information in a confident manner – NOT in a cocky way. 
 
 Sticking to your facts but not forcing them on anyone. Keeping dignified! 
 
 Giving other people the impression you know what you are talking about!. (If you don’t know what you are talking about – FIND OUT). 
 
 Be confident but stay professional, respectful and helpful 
 
 Ask the right questions in the manner that will purge the information you want and need to move forward……… 
 Communicate effectively!

6. Conflict Resolution 
 Be a Referee? 
 Look at both sides of the story 
 What does the customer want? 
 What does the business want? 
 ‘I just want my car fixing’ 
 ‘We just want more time to fix it!’ 
 
 What are the options? – Give at least 5 answers……………………

What are the options? 
 Lend the customer a car? 
 Persuade him the job is better not rushed – (right first time) 
 Drive him home and pick him up when fully ready 
 Offer repair at reduced cost 
 Offer a extended guarantee 
 Explain comprehensively why the delay to the customer’s satisfaction 
 Be assertive, friendly, think of customer repeat business and word of mouth and most of all – Keep Your Promises.

7. Empathy 
 
 Empathy??????? 
 
 ‘Research’ Empathy ……… 
 
 What does Empathy mean? 
 
 Is it the same as Sympathy?

Empathy 
 
 Empathy is not saying ‘I’m really sorry’ just for the sake of it, that’s Sympathy, some people like to hear someone agreeing with their problem, that’s Sympathy….customers and friends don’t want Sympathy they want Empathy. 
 Empathy is ‘understanding’…..really ‘ getting’ what the customer/friend is feeling, understanding their current emotions…. 
 ’Why is it happening to me? – why is it happening again? - Is somebody winding me up? – I can’t afford it! – I don’t understand what’s going on!

8. Depersonalisation

 Ever been on a tour with a group and hear the same dialogue by someone else, a script?….. depersonalization! 
 It doesn’t work – it’s false – It’s so easy to spot! 
 Does she know what she’s talking about?

10. A Sense of Humour 
 
 Having a sense of humour is appreciating the ‘moment’ and at a certain point, (this doesn't always happen), make light of a situation. 
 Be very careful….having a sense of humour is picking the moment, it’s a skill. 
 Having a sense of humour is not being the Joker, it’s not having a laugh at the person’s expense or experience….it can soon backfire! 
 It can also mean, accept comments from the ‘victim’ – don’t get upset about it! 
 Deal with it ……
