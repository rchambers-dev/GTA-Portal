 Topic
 Fasteners, Technical Drawings, Fabrication and Safe Tool Use
 Occupational relevance: This session develops recognition of fasteners, following drawings, safe hand/tool use, marking out, measurement, tolerance, teamwork and housekeeping. Pathway examples include service fasteners, heavy-duty fixings, trim and panel fasteners, rivets/bonding preparations and wheel/security components.
 Lesson aims
 • Recognise common fasteners, washers and their purposes.
 • Follow a simple engineering drawing and risk-controlled process.
 • Develop safe fabrication, measurement and quality-checking habits.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Select a suitable fastener or fixing for a stated purpose.
 • Interpret dimensions, datum and tolerance from a drawing.
 • Complete a supervised fabrication/equivalent task safely and accurately.
 • Record quality findings and improvements.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Fastener identification and technical-drawing questions.
 • During: Observation of risk assessment, tool use and measurement.
 • End: Quality check against drawing plus concise technical write-up.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
