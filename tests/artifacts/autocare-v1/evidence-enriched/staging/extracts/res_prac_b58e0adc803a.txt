Your task
A vehicle has arrived with a tyre pressure warning lamp illuminated. You must inspect tyre condition and
pressures, identify the TPMS type where possible, carry out a reset/relearn where appropriate and record
customer-facing findings.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and confirm the TPMS warning concern.
2 Check tyre pressures and inspect tyre condition.
3 Identify TPMS system type and reset/relearn as instructed.
4 Record findings, pressures and any customer recommendations.
5 Reinstate the vehicle and workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle with TPMS
• Tyre pressure gauge
• TPMS tool or scan tool if available
• Manufacturer technical information
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
