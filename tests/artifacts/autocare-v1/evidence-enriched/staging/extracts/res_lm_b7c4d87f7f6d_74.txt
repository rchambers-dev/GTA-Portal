Auto-care Parts Task Help Sheet
 Part 1.
 Get GTA fast parts paperwork with parts info (box number matches invoice number)
 Check parts on list vs what is in the box. (Tutor to make sure it’s right box)
 It’s a good idea to tick off items as you go.
 Identify the issue with one of the parts. ( make notes on invoice of said issue)
 Book into parts system but leave out the incorrect part.
 Explain to assessor the issue and how it needs to be resolved.
 Part 2
 You must now book parts onto the laptop spreadsheet on “quantity in column” .
 Check prices om invoice vs the spreadsheet
 Ensure you can explain findings and have ability to print or screen shot.
 Do not save the sheet, just exit the sheet.

 Part 3
 Refer to the spread sheet for stock location and place correctly into cupboard
 Leave the incorrect part out ready for a return.
 Close spread sheet but again do not save it.

 Part 4
 Load up customer invoice. The assessor will now ask for a part that you do not have in stock.
 Look up the requested part in the Moog parts book ( e.g., track rod end etc)
 Note** Parts book is in manufacturer/model alphabetical order.
 Now there is a roll play of a phone call for getting price/availability for said part.
 Ensure you have trade/retail prices for the part and make a note of it .
 Create an invoice using info collected from book/phone call. ( All prices are Ex VAT, system adds it on at the bottom of spread sheet for you, so you do not need to worry)
