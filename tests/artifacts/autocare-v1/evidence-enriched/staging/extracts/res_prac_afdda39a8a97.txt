Your task
A hybrid or electric vehicle/rig is being used for awareness training. You must identify visible EV or hybrid
components, explain their function and show safe non-invasive working boundaries.
What you must do
1 Prepare the work area and confirm the vehicle/rig is safe for awareness activity.
2 Identify visible EV/hybrid components as directed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
3 Explain the basic function and risks of selected components.
4 Record any safety warnings, limitations and referral requirements.
5 Reinstate the vehicle/rig and workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Hybrid/electric vehicle or training rig
• Manufacturer safety information
• HV awareness PPE where appropriate
• Warning label examples
• Apprentice Assessment Record
