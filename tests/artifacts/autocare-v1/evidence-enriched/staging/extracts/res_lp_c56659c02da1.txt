 Topic and intended learning
 Lesson aims - Develop secure occupational knowledge and safe working practice for final capstone, evidence quality and gateway readiness. - Connect the session to workplace quality, communication and customer/business impact. - Produce clear evidence that can be reviewed and improved.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to - Explain the key principles, sequence, risks and controls for final capstone, evidence quality and gateway readiness. - Complete the planned practical, scenario or documentation activity safely and accurately. - Record findings and justify decisions using technical information or workplace procedures.
 How learning will be checked - Baseline and retrieval questions at the start. - Observation, questioning and review during activity. - Feedback, correction and an end-of-session impact check. - Formal assessment, gateway and EPA activity remain separate.
 Inclusion, support and stretch - Use visual prompts, glossaries, staged checklists and model examples where needed. - Adjust access arrangements without lowering occupational expectations. - Stretch: justify alternatives, evaluate risk/quality and explain workplace impact.
 RPL and individual training plan control - Deliver the full master plan unless an approved RPL adjustment changes the learner’s block plan. - Record only the eligible OTJ actually delivered. Omitted or shortened learning must agree with Excel and the portal.
 Impact and follow-up - Identify what the apprentice can now explain or complete with greater confidence and independence. - Record one workplace application or evidence action. - Complete the Apprenticeship Block Review and Training Impact Record.
