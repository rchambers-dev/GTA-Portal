 Topic
 Tools and Safety Consolidation, Basic Circuits and Relay Awareness
 Occupational relevance: This session consolidates health and safety and tool knowledge, then develops basic series/parallel circuit and relay awareness. The learning supports electrical understanding in service pathways and safe recognition of electrical controls, interlocks and components in bodyshop and refinishing environments.
 Lesson aims
 • Consolidate health, safety, tools and equipment knowledge.
 • Understand the difference between simple series and parallel circuits.
 • Build and test a low-voltage circuit safely, including relay awareness.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Explain series and parallel circuit characteristics.
 • Draw and build a simple low-voltage circuit from a diagram.
 • Identify basic relay purpose and terminal information.
 • Use a multimeter to complete and record a safe circuit check.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Retrieval questions and short H&S/tools recap before formal tests.
 • During: Circuit-diagram questioning, observed build and troubleshooting.
 • End: Working circuit, measurement record and corrected written explanation.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
