Your task
A vehicle has excessive or uneven tyre wear. You must inspect the tyres and related steering/suspension
geometry, record evidence and identify the likely root cause before recommending work.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and complete tyre/alignment pre-checks.
2 Record tyre wear patterns and pressures.
3 Measure or interpret alignment/geometry data as instructed.
4 Identify likely root cause and recommended action.
5 Record findings and reinstate the workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle
• Tread depth gauge and tyre pressure gauge
• Alignment equipment or sample printouts
• Manufacturer geometry data
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
