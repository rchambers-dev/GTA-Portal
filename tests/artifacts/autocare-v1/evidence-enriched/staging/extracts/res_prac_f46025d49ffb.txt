Your task
A vehicle has an engine management concern. You must carry out a basic non-invasive sensor check using a scan
tool or suitable method, record outputs and explain the evidence and next steps.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and confirm the reported symptom.
2 Locate the relevant sensor/component as instructed.
3 Check sensor output or scan data using suitable equipment.
4 Record results and explain findings to the assessor/workplace mentor.
5 Reinstate the vehicle/workstation and complete documentation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or engine management rig
• Scan tool or multimeter as instructed
• Manufacturer technical information
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
