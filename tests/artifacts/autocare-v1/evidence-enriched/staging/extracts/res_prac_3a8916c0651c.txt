Your task
A customer reports uneven tyre wear and the steering wheel is off-centre. You must carry out alignment
pre-checks, record geometry readings and explain whether adjustment or further repair is required.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and alignment equipment safely.
2 Carry out pre-checks including tyre pressure, tyre condition and suspension condition.
3 Measure alignment readings and compare with manufacturer data.
4 Adjust if instructed and within competence, or explain referral/action required.
5 Record findings and reinstate the vehicle/workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle
• Wheel alignment equipment
• Tyre pressure gauge and tread depth gauge
• Manufacturer geometry data
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
