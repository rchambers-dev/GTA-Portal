Your task
A customer reports poor cabin cooling or demisting. You must complete non-invasive
air-conditioning/climate-control checks, record visible findings and identify when F-gas or specialist referral is
required.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and confirm the customer concern.
2 Carry out visible and functional checks as instructed.
3 Record refrigerant type/quantity information from technical data where available.
4 Identify checks that require qualified F-gas personnel.
5 Explain findings and reinstate the vehicle/workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or AC training rig
• Manufacturer AC information
• Thermometer or scan tool if available
• PPE and warning information
• Apprentice Assessment Record
