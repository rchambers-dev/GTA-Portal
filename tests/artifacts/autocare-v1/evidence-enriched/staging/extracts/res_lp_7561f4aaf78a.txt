 Topic
 Multimeter Use, Electrical Measurement and Fabrication Quality
 Occupational relevance: This session develops safe multimeter inspection and basic voltage/resistance measurement, interpretation of results, continued accurate fabrication and quality control. Examples include 12V light/autocare systems, 24V heavy systems, bodyshop electrical safety and paint-booth/equipment controls.
 Lesson aims
 • Develop safe basic multimeter function and calibration checks.
 • Record and interpret simple voltage and resistance readings.
 • Reinforce accurate practical work, tolerance, teamwork and quality.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Inspect and prepare a multimeter safely.
 • Obtain and record simple voltage and resistance readings.
 • Explain the limits of basic testing and when to escalate.
 • Complete or quality-check the fabrication/equivalent task against tolerance.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Function/lead/range identification and safety questions.
 • During: Observed measurement and quality checks with immediate feedback.
 • End: Completed reading record and quality reflection.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.
