 Topic and intended learning
 Lesson aims - Develop secure occupational knowledge and safe working practice for advanced diagnostic process and confirmation of root cause. - Connect the session to workplace quality, communication and customer/business impact. - Produce clear evidence that can be reviewed and improved.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to - Explain the key principles, sequence, risks and controls for advanced diagnostic process and confirmation of root cause. - Complete the planned practical, scenario or documentation activity safely and accurately. - Record findings and justify decisions using technical information or workplace procedures.
 How learning will be checked - Baseline and retrieval questions at the start. - Observation, questioning and review during activity. - Feedback, correction and an end-of-session impact check. - Formal assessment, gateway and EPA activity remain separate.
 Inclusion, support and stretch - Use visual prompts, glossaries, staged checklists and model examples where needed. - Adjust access arrangements without lowering occupational expectations. - Stretch: justify alternatives, evaluate risk/quality and explain workplace impact.
 RPL and individual training plan control - Deliver the full master plan unless an approved RPL adjustment changes the learner’s block plan. - Record only the eligible OTJ actually delivered. Omitted or shortened learning must agree with Excel and the portal.
 Impact and follow-up - Identify what the apprentice can now explain or complete with greater confidence and independence. - Record one workplace application or evidence action. - Review progress against the block objectives and agree the next step.

 Tutor-led session developing the advanced diagnostic process, with emphasis on confirming the root cause rather than only identifying symptoms. The tutor explains how a technician moves from customer concern to initial inspection, then to structured testing, evidence gathering and confirmation.
 Baseline questions; correct misconceptions. Support with visuals/glossary. Stretch: connect principles to workplace risk and quality.

 Guided practical or scenario-based diagnostic activity using vehicles, training rigs or structured case studies. Learners begin by confirming the reported concern, then identify the systems most likely to be involved.
 Observe process and safe working. Support with staged checklist. Stretch: justify method and compare alternatives.

 12:45-14:15 1.50 OTJ
 Application activity where learners complete an advanced diagnostic record showing how they confirmed or ruled out possible causes. Learners must document the original symptom, the checks carried out, the results obtained and the reasoning that led to the final conclusion.
 Review output and reasoning. Support with model examples. Stretch: evaluate quality, efficiency and customer/business impact.
 Inspection rigs or vehicles; checklists; diagnostic aids; PPE

 14:30-16:00 1.50 OTJ
 Consolidation through group review of diagnostic pathways and root-cause decisions. Learners compare approaches and identify where steps could have been missed or where evidence was not strong enough. The tutor reinforces the importance of confirmation checks before recommending repair.
 Exit check and feedback. Confirm evidence action. Stretch: explain improvement and transfer to the workplace.
 Evidence: Inspection sheets; defect photos; annotated checklists
 Workplace follow-up Apply one aspect of the session in the workplace and retain a job card, observation, photograph, mentor comment or other suitable evidence where available.
