Your task
A vehicle has steering play or a worn steering component. You must inspect the relevant area, remove/refit the
component as instructed, record technical data and explain the customer recommendations.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle and confirm safe lifting/support arrangements.
2 Inspect steering components and identify the fault.
3 Remove and refit the component instructed by the assessor/workplace mentor.
4 Record torque settings and any alignment requirement.
5 Reinstate the vehicle and explain customer recommendations.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or steering rig
• Replacement steering component where required
• Manufacturer technical information
• Torque wrench and general tools
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
