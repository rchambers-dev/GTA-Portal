Types of Toe 
 Next > 
 Toe is the angle that each front wheel makes with the longitudinal axis of the vehicle when the steering is at its central position. 
 Ideally toe should be zero, but toe angles tend to change when the vehicle is moving due to dynamic forces. 
 Vehicle manufacturers specify the correct toe for when the vehicle is stationary.

Toe In and Toe Out 
 Next > 
 When the vehicle is moving, road friction pushes the wheels back to zero toe. 
 A rear wheel drive vehicle is usually set to have a slight toe-in. 
 A front wheel drive vehicle is usually set to have a slight toe-out.

Scrub Radius (Steering Offset) 
 Positive scrub radius is when the center of the tire is outboard of the steering axis. It makes it easier to turn the steering when stationary. 
 Negative scrub radius is when the center of the tire is inboard of the steering axis. It improves stability under braking. 
 Next > 
 The lateral distance from where the steering axis intersects the ground and the center of the tire is called the scrub radius.
