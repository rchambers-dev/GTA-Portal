 RPL and portal adjustment control
 Equal-KSB OTJ unit value and adjustment method The authoritative primary-block allocation is held in the controlled Autocare ST0499 v1.3 RPL matrix for the applicable cohort; its filename and version must be recorded at programme approval and kept aligned with this SOW.
 ST0499 v1.3 contains 64 unique published KSB codes: 34 knowledge, 25 skills and 5 behaviours. The equal planned OTJ unit value is 611.50 / 64 = 9.5546875 hours per KSB (store 9.5547; display 9.55). Each KSB is listed once in the controlled RPL matrix and assigned to one primary block. Raw block/group OTJ is the number of primary KSBs multiplied by the unrounded unit value. It is then converted to the approved delivery pattern; session rounding and final rebalancing can make the displayed timetabled hours differ from the raw equal-share figure. Foundation Skills retains its controlled variable-hour sessions. Weekly KSB references show teaching or reinforcement only and must not be counted again.

 Source standard
 Autocare Technician ST0499 v1.3.
 Official planning data
 Typical apprenticeship duration 30 months; published minimum 605 hours; maximum funding band £13,000. The controlled SOW gateway/EPA period is one month and must be checked against the applicable assessment plan.
 Planned SOW
 112 calendar delivery weeks; 113 planned learning sessions; 611.50 eligible OTJ; 6.50-hour headroom. Week 112 contains two separately timetabled specialist sessions.
 Calendar feasibility
 112 / 46.4 x 12 = 28.97 indicative practical months; plus 1.00 gateway/EPA month = 29.97 months; displayed apprenticeship end Month 30. Actual dates remain the authoritative validation.
 KSB and RPL control
 64 unique published KSBs are counted once in the controlled RPL matrix and assigned to one primary block. Equal OTJ unit value = 611.50 / 64 = 9.5546875 hours (store 9.5547; display 9.55). Raw block/group OTJ = primary KSB count x the unrounded unit value. Weekly repetitions are teaching/reinforcement links only. No manual KSB or block/group complexity weighting is permitted.
 Funding control
 Minimum price reduction = programme RPL % x 50%. The final negotiated price must not exceed the RPL-adjusted funding cap; TNP1 plus TNP2 must reconcile to the final price.
 Review triggers
 Change to the standard/EPA plan, funding-rule set, delivery calendar, KSB matrix, Foundation Skills pack, equipment or employer requirements.
 Workbook and portal dependency
 The associated Autocare RPL workbook, training plan and portal must use this 112-calendar-week / 113-session / 611.50-hour master, equal-KSB calculations and the Month 29 practical / Month 30 apprenticeship end points.
