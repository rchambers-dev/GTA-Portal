 Critical control. This is an on-programme transfer between two curriculum structures for the same apprenticeship standard. It is not a new recognition of prior learning exercise. Do not change the apprentice's original RPL decision, negotiated price or funding solely because the SOW changes. Preserve valid learning and assessment evidence, carry forward actual OTJ once, and plan only the remaining learning and assessment.

 1. Purpose and required outcome
 This guide allows GTA staff to identify what an apprentice has already learned and evidenced under the Group model, map that evidence to the receiving Blocks, identify genuine gaps and agree a controlled forward plan. It prevents unnecessary repetition while ensuring that no Block is signed off from Group completion alone.
 2. Transfer principles
 Step
 Principle
 Required treatment
 1
 Preserve the learner record
 Retain the original start date, initial assessment, RPL record, training plan, agreed price, actual OTJ and all Group/CEA evidence.
 2
 Map evidence, not labels
 A completed Group is evidence for one or more Blocks. It is not automatically equivalent to a whole Block because Blocks integrate several duties and technical areas.
 3
 No double counting
 Do not record the same learning hours again. Historical OTJ remains actual OTJ; only new Block learning completed after transfer creates additional actual OTJ.
 4
 Assess only the gaps
 Where valid evidence meets a Block outcome, carry it forward. Deliver and assess only missing, weak, outdated or insufficiently evidenced outcomes.
 5
 Keep assessment separate
 CEA, knowledge-check, formal Block assessment, progress review, gateway and EPA time remain non-OTJ. Corrective teaching can be eligible when it is substantive new occupational learning and is separately recorded.
 6
 Approve the decision
 The tutor/assessor records the evidence and gaps; the curriculum lead or IQA samples the mapping; the apprentice and employer agree the revised plan.

 3. Transition process
 Step
 Action
 What must be recorded
 Owner
 1
 Freeze and export the Group record
 Record completed/in-progress Groups, weeks delivered, CEA attempts and results, knowledge checks, job cards, e-logbook evidence, employer evidence and actual OTJ at the transfer date.
 Tutor/assessor
 2
 Map evidence to Blocks
 Use Sections 4–6. Link each evidence item to the receiving Block outcome/KSB. Record the source and date; do not rely on a Group title alone.
 Tutor/assessor
 3
 Complete the Block gap review
 For each Block rate evidence as Met, Partially met or Not yet evidenced. Identify missing knowledge, practical competence, behaviours and documentation.
 Tutor/assessor
 4
 Set the receiving Block position
 Choose Transferred complete, Partially transferred or Full Block required using the decision rules in Section 7.
 Tutor/assessor
 5
 Agree targeted learning and assessment
 Schedule only outstanding learning and the receiving Block assessment. Preserve the planned practical end and Month 30 apprenticeship end where feasible.
 Curriculum lead
 6
 Update controlled systems
 Update the training plan, portal/tracker, OTJ plan and next review. Lock the signed transition record and retain the outgoing Group evidence.
 Administration/tutor
 7
 Approve and review
 Obtain apprentice, employer, tutor/assessor and curriculum lead/IQA approval. Review the transition at the next progress review.
 GTA/apprentice/employer

 Placement rule. Do not place an apprentice into a Block merely by matching their calendar week. The Group and Block SOWs sequence technical content differently. Placement must follow mapped evidence and the agreed gap plan.

 5. Detailed Group evidence transfer reference
 Outgoing Group
 Likely receiving Blocks
 Evidence to transfer
 Required Block treatment
 G1 – Foundation Skills
 B1 direct; B2–B10 supporting
 FS01–FS10 learning records, observations, safety/tool/electrical/measurement evidence and transition record.
 B1 may transfer as complete only where all ten controlled sessions and required evidence are present. Carry the evidence forward as foundation/reinforcement elsewhere.
 G2 – Electric and Hybrid Awareness
 B4 and B9 direct; B2, B6, B7 and B10 supporting
 Knowledge check; EV/hybrid identification; component-location CEA; charging/customer handover and safe referral evidence.
 Credit evidenced awareness and safe-boundary outcomes. Check gaps in applied diagnostics, charging, ADAS/hydrogen and integrated workshop application before completing B4 or B9.
 G3 – Wheels and Tyres
 B2 and B3 direct; B4, B7–B10 supporting
 Tyre/wheel CEAs, TPMS, balance, puncture, torque, inspection sheets, job cards and legal/serviceability knowledge.
 Carry practical evidence to tyre, inspection, workshop-process and pre-calibration outcomes. Assess any missing integrated customer, diagnostic or documentation elements.
 G4 – Basic Braking
 B2 and B3 direct; B5, B7, B8 and B10 supporting
 Brake CEAs, measurements, hydraulic work, component job cards, service verification and knowledge check.
 Use for basic brake inspection/repair outcomes. Advanced diagnosis, ABS/data interpretation and integrated Block decisions remain separate unless evidenced.
 G5 – Basic Steering and Suspension
 B4 and B5 direct; B2, B7–B10 supporting
 Alignment and component CEAs, safe spring/lifting evidence, measurements, job cards and technical questioning.
 Credit basic geometry and component work. Check advanced alignment, ride-height/ADAS interaction, diagnostic reasoning and integrated reporting gaps.
 G6 – Cooling, Lubrication, and Filters
 B3 and B5 direct; B7, B8 and B10 supporting
 Cooling/lubrication CEAs, leak/function checks, fluid handling, waste records, job cards and knowledge check.
 Credit service, repair, environmental and process outcomes. Check system integration, diagnosis, customer reporting and capstone application.
 G7 – Starting and Charging Systems
 B2, B5 and B6 direct; B7–B10 supporting
 Battery/starter/alternator CEAs, test results, voltage-drop records, job cards and safe-procedure evidence.
 Credit evidenced low-voltage testing and repair. Check advanced diagnosis, EV boundaries, documentation and integrated inspection outcomes.
 G8 – Customer Service and Sales
 B2, B3, B7 and B8 direct; B4–B6, B9 and B10 supporting
 Customer CEA, intake/handover records, complaints, quotations, authorisation, written/digital communication and compliance evidence.
 Carry communication and commercial evidence across Blocks. Technical Block completion still requires the relevant occupational knowledge and practical evidence.
 G9 – Stock Management
 B3 and B8 direct; B2, B4–B7, B9 and B10 supporting
 Parts-sourcing CEA, orders, goods-in, stock counts, variance/returns/warranty records and supplier communications.
 Credit parts, stock, quotation support and audit-trail outcomes. Do not use stock evidence to complete unrelated technical outcomes.
 G10 – Advanced Braking
 B5–B8 direct; B2, B3 and B10 supporting
 ABS CEAs, sensor/reluctor evidence, scan data, fault codes, oscilloscope/multimeter results, verification and road-test information.
 Credit advanced brake diagnostic outcomes where evidence is current and authentic. Complete any Block-specific customer, parts, safety and integrated workflow gaps.
 G11 – Advanced Steering and Suspension
 B4, B5 and B7–B9 direct; B2 and B10 supporting
 Traction/ride-height/adaptive/geometry/ADAS CEAs, alignment reports, diagnostic plans, referral and evidence-quality records.
 Credit advanced geometry, system interaction and pre-calibration outcomes. Check Block-specific EV, workshop organisation, customer and capstone gaps.
 G12 – Exhaust and Emissions
 B5–B7 and B9 direct; B3, B8 and B10 supporting
 Exhaust/emissions CEAs, analyser/data evidence, oxygen/EGR/catalyst/DPF awareness, fault diagnosis and environmental records.
 Credit exhaust, emissions, environmental and diagnostic outcomes. Check wider system integration and Block-specific documentation/customer outcomes.
 G13 – Air Conditioning
 B6 and B10 direct; B7–B9 supporting
 HVAC CEAs, temperature/system checks, component job cards, scan data, electrified-vehicle heat-pump and referral evidence.
 Credit inspection/component and climate-control outcomes. F-gas certification or refrigerant handling must not be inferred unless separately evidenced and authorised.
 G14 – Vehicle Inspection
 B2, B3, B5, B7, B8 and B10 direct; B4, B6 and B9 supporting
 Service/safety-inspection CEAs, job cards, defect prioritisation, customer report/quote, diagnostic results, capstone, gap plan and employer evidence.
 Use as the main integrated evidence source. Complete any specialist Block gaps and the receiving Block assessment before sign-off.

 7. Block assessment method after transfer
 The receiving Block assessment decision must use a combined evidence set. Existing Group evidence is carried forward where it is valid; the assessor then completes only the missing learning and assessment.
 Stage
 Assessment component
 Required evidence/action
 OTJ treatment
 1
 Evidence transfer review
 Verify identity, date, authenticity and result of CEAs, knowledge checks, job cards, observations, e-logbook and employer evidence. Link each item to Block outcomes/KSBs.
 Non-OTJ administration/review
 2
 Block diagnostic/gap check
 Use questioning, document review and practical discussion/observation to identify what is Met, Partially met or Not yet evidenced. This is not a new RPL score.
 Assessment/review – non-OTJ
 3
 Targeted Block learning
 Deliver missing or weak knowledge, skills and behaviours through the receiving Block SOW. Record only new substantive occupational learning as OTJ.
 Eligible OTJ where rules are met
 4
 Ongoing formative checks
 Use observation, questioning, job-card/document review, feedback, correction and repeated practice during Block delivery.
 Training; eligible only where substantive learning occurs
 5
 Separate formal Block assessment
 Where scheduled, complete the formal practical/knowledge assessment separately from the 6.00-hour GLH/OTJ session. Retain the assessment record and outcome.
 Non-OTJ assessment
 6
 Block reflection and evidence review
 Apprentice records what they can now do, evidence produced and remaining development. Tutor and employer/mentor feedback confirm workplace transfer where available.
 Review/reflection; classify by actual activity
 7
 Completion decision
 Confirm that required Block outcomes and its primary KSB evidence are complete. Record Transferred complete, Completed after targeted learning, or Further action required.
 Non-OTJ decision/sign-off

 8. Status and percentage rules
 Receiving status
 Decision rule
 Required action
 Transferred complete
 Mapped Group evidence already covers all controlled Block outcomes/primary KSB evidence and has been verified.
 No repeat training. Record the evidence references and approval date; complete only administrative transfer.
 Partially transferred
 Some Block outcomes are evidenced but one or more knowledge, skill, behaviour or integrated application gaps remain.
 Carry forward the verified evidence; schedule targeted learning and the relevant Block assessment.
 Full Block required
 Evidence is missing, insufficient, unauthenticated, out of date for the intended use, or does not cover the receiving Block.
 Deliver and assess the Block normally. Preserve historical evidence but do not use it to complete the Block.
 Operational Block progress % (if required by portal)
 Use the controlled Autocare ST0499 v1.3 primary-block RPL matrix for the applicable cohort. Equal-weight the primary KSB assessment units assigned once to that Block: fully met primary KSB units / total primary KSB units in the Block x 100.
 Record the controlled matrix filename and version. This is an on-programme progress indicator only: do not treat it as a new KSB RPL score, recalculate programme RPL, change funding or alter the original planned OTJ baseline.
