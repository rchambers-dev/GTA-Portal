Your task
A vehicle has an ABS or traction warning lamp. You must carry out a structured inspection and test of the wheel
speed sensor circuit or output, record evidence and explain the likely fault or referral route.
What you must do
1 Prepare the vehicle/rig and confirm the warning lamp or fault condition.
2 Read and record relevant DTCs if equipment is available.
3 Inspect the wheel speed sensor, wiring and reluctor/tone ring as instructed.
4 Check output using a suitable method and record results.
5 Explain findings and reinstate the vehicle/workstation.
What good evidence looks like
SAFE SETUP
Suitable PPE, a prepared work area and controlled vehicle or
equipment.
CORRECT METHOD
A logical sequence using the relevant procedure and technical
information.
ACCURATE EVIDENCE
Actual readings, findings and actions recorded clearly and
precisely.
SOUND DECISIONS
Appropriate recommendations, referral or authorisation
supported by evidence.
You will need
• Light vehicle or ABS training rig
• Scan tool, multimeter or oscilloscope if available
• Manufacturer technical information
• PPE and Apprentice Assessment Record
