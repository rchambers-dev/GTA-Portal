 Topic
 Hybrid and High-Voltage Vehicle Awareness
 Occupational relevance: This session develops recognition of electrified-vehicle hazards, safe approach, competence boundaries and escalation. The depth of work differs by pathway, but every apprentice must recognise high-voltage vehicles and avoid unsafe preparation, repair, inspection or refinishing activity.
 Lesson aims
 • Introduce hybrid and electric vehicle identification and warning systems.
 • Develop safe approach, key-control and exclusion principles.
 • Clarify limits of competence and correct escalation routes.
 By the end, apprentices should be able to
 • Identify common hybrid/EV labels, components and high-voltage colours.
 • Explain at least four significant hazards and suitable controls.
 • Complete a safe-approach checklist for a vehicle or scenario.
 • State when work must stop and an authorised person must be involved.
 How learning will be checked
 • Start: Short image-based identification task and prior-knowledge questions.
 • During: Scenario questioning and observation of safe approach.
 • End: Completed checklist and exit explanation of competence boundaries.
 Simple impact measure
 • Compare the starting-point response with the completed end task.
 • Record: Not yet secure / Developing / Secure.
 • Set one follow-up action only where further support or stretch is needed.

 • Prepare pathway-specific scenarios for the cohort.
 • Confirm barriers, signage and emergency arrangements.
