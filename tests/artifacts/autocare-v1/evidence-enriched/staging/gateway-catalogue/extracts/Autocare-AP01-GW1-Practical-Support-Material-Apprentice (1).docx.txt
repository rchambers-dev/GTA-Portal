 Objectives:
 To ensure safe working practices are followed throughout your assessment
 To demonstrate a methodical procedure when carrying out removal and replacement of wheels and tyres
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to inspect wheels and tyres
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to measure tyre tread depth
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to reinstate the vehicle to the pre-assessment condition
 To correctly complete the Apprentice Assessment Record including details specific to the system worked on during your assessment

 Materials you need at the workstation:
 Light v ehicle (not rig)
 Tyre removal and balancing equipment
 Tyre pressure gauge
 Manufacturer’s instructions and technical information relevant for the assessment
 General and specialist tools and equipment made available by your assessor relevant for the task
 Apprentice Assessment Record sheet

 Scenario:
 You are required to carry out a wheel and tyre inspection, remove balance and replace a set of front wheels and tyres .

 Complete the following tasks and note your results where indicated:

 Ensure your working environment is safe and the vehicle is correctl y prepared before starting work

 Carry out a wheel and tyre inspecti on including measurements of tyre tread and pressure

 Remove and replace both front wheels

 Remove, replace and balance both front tyres

 Tighten the wheel nuts to 50% (half) of the torque setting specified ensuring you record the CORRECT settings in the Apprentice Assessment Record sheet

 Reinstate the vehicle and work station to a pre-assessment condition
