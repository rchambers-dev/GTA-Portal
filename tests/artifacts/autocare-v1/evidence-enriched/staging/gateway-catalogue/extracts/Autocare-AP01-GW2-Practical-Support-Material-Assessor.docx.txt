 Emissions Related Fault
 This purpose of this document is to support the assessor in setting up the workstation and vehicle prior to the A pprentice ’s Gateway 2 practical assessment.

 You must provide the Apprentice with the Emissions Related Fault instructions and Apprentice Assessment Record. Apprentice s must complete the task and documentation fully to enable the assessor to make a judgement on competency; this task is graded pass or refer and there is a time limit of 9 0 minutes.

 Included in this document :
 Workstation set- up requirements including tools and equipment
 Vehicle set- up prior to assessment
 Assessor Record and comments sheet

 Materials required at the workstation:
 Light Vehicle
 Relevant workshop manuals and technical equipment for the vehicle
 General hand tools
 Emission testing equipment
 Diagnostic scan tool with live data capability
 Vehicle Protective Equipment (VPE)
 Personal Protection Equipment (PPE)
 Apprentice ’s instructions
 Assessor Instructions and Assessor Record

 Workstation set- up required:
 Vehicle must be positioned in a suitable location in the workshop NOT raised off the ground.

 Vehicle to be set with a fault that causes the vehicle emissions to fall outside of manufacturer’s specifications. This fault should be set so that the component or the signal to/ from the component is interrupted causing the component not to function. This must not be a disconnected connector or made obvious to the A pprentice .

 The A pprentice must:
 R aise the vehicle off the ground and stabilise
 C arry out an inspection of the vehicle emissions and interpret t he results against manufacturer ’s specification
 U se diagnostic equipment to retrieve faults codes and investigate the operation of suspected faulty components
 U se live data to confirm the fault, this does not need to be rectified
 R eplace parts following manufacturer’s instructions, bolts to be torqued to 50% of specified figure
 L ower vehicle to the ground and clean work area , returning all tools and equipment
