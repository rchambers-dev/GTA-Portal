 Emissions Related F ault

 Objectives:

 To demonstrate a methodical procedure when carrying out your diagnosis
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to use diagnostic equipment and live data
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to use emission testing equipment and interpret results
 To demonstrate your knowledge and ability to reinstate the vehicle to the pre-assessment condition
 To correctly complete the Apprentice Assessment Record including details specific to the system worked on during your assessment

 Materials you need at the workstation:
 Vehicle (not rig)
 Emission testing equipment
 Diagnostic scan tool with live data capability
 Manufacturer’s instructions and technical information relevant for the assessment
 General and specialist tools and equipment made available by your assessor relevant for the task
 Apprentice Assessment Record sheet

 Scenario:
 You are required to diagnose an emission system related fault using emission testing and diagnostic equipment.

 Complete the following tasks and note your results where indicated:

 Ensure your working environment is safe and the vehicle is correctl y prepared before starting work

 Carry out a vehicle emissions test and interpret the results

 Use a diagnostic tool to retrieve faults codes and observe live data to verify the fault

 Report your findings and rec ommendations for repair to your assessor

 Tighten all components to 50% (half) of the torque setting specified ensuring you record the CORRECT settings in the Apprentice Assessment Record sheet

 Reinst ate the vehicle and work area to a pre-assessment condition
