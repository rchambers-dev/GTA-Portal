===== SLIDE 2 =====
Introduction

Tyres are the only part of a vehicle that make contact with the ground.

They cushion the wheels from the road and provide grip and traction.

Tyres are made of natural rubber or synthetic neoprene.

Tyre Types

Cross ply tyres are designed with criss-crossed sidewall plies, running diagonally from bead to bead.

Radial tyres are designed with radial cords that run straight across the tyre, from bead to bead. So 90 degrees to the tread direction.

Special Service Tyre or Space Saver

This is a space-saver spare tyre; it is used to replace a flat tyre in an emergency.

It is only for temporary use and should not be included when rotating tyres.

===== SLIDE 11 =====
Tubeless Tyres

Due to cost and reliability, most vehicles are fitted with tubeless tyres.

A tyre can be either a tube type or tubeless type construction.

A tubeless tyre holds the air pressure in the wheel by sealing the tyre onto the rim around the circumference.

The tubeless tyre is inflated by forcing air through a valve fitted to the wheel rim.
