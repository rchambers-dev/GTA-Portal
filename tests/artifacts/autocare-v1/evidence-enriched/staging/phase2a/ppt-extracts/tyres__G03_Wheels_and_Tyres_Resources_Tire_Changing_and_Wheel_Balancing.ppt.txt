===== SLIDE 2 =====
Wheel and Tire Runout

Runout causes vibration that can usually be felt through the steering wheel.

Next >

Tire runout is a lack of roundness of a tire.

Wheel runout is caused by distortion of a wheel rim.

===== SLIDE 3 =====
Runout Causes

Lateral runout is measured by placing a dial indicator against the sidewall or rim.

Runout can be caused by a faulty wheel or tire, impact damage, or fatigue.

Next >

Runout results should be compared with specifications in the service manual.

Radial runout is measured using a roller indicator on the tread or inside of the rim.

A runout problem can be difficult to rectify.

===== SLIDE 4 =====
Wheel and Tire Balance

When a wheel is out of balance, the tire vibrates up and down, and or side to side.

Imbalance can be overcome by static or dynamic balance.

The weight of the tire and wheel should be evenly distributed around the axis of rotation.

Wheel weights

Balancing a Wheel and Tire Assembly

A static balance identifies the heavy point of a wheel and tire assembly.

A dynamic balance uses a wheel balancing machine to check a spinning wheel and tire assembly for vibration.

Extra weight is placed directly opposite the heavy point to correct the problem.

Next >

The weights are then manually added to the wheel rim.

The machine tells the operator the position and size of the correcting weights.

===== SLIDE 12 =====
Off-car Wheel and Tire Balancing

The wheel and tire assembly is removed from a vehicle and mounted on a balancing machine.

The machine operator must follow set procedures, based on the machine type and shop safety rules.

Next >

Always spin the wheel and check the balance again after fitting the weights.

Wheel balancing machines normally calculate the size and the place for the weights to be added.

Lug Nut Torque

The correct torque of wheel fixings is vital for all vehicles.

Low torque may cause wheels to come off.

Excessive torque canlead to distortionand vibration.

Next >

Lug nuts should be tightened in a diagonal pattern.

===== SLIDE 15 =====
Tire Changing Safety Precautions

You must follow all safety precautions when changing a tire:

Wear eye protection

Remove the valve core before breaking the bead away from the rim

Keep fingers clear when removing a tire from the wheel

Next >

Ensure that the rim is smooth and clean before re-fitting

Lubricate the bead and rim with vegetable oil-soap solution(Never use engine oil or antifreeze)

Never inflate a tire when it is lying on the floor. It can jump dangerously

Stand clear when inflating. A loud “pop” will be heard as the beads seat

Keep pressure below the maximum stated on the sidewall of the tire
